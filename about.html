Who we are

Family-Run, Traveller-Loved

Hirusha Travels is a family-run travel agency based in Kurunegala, Sri Lanka. For over 10 years we have helped thousands of travellers enjoy safe, comfortable and memorable holidays, with licensed guides, trusted hotels and personal service.

We know the island because we live here. That is why our packages, from golden beaches and misty tea country to ancient cities and wild safaris, are chosen by local experts and planned around you.

  • SLTDA licensed agency
  • Licensed guides and trusted hotels
  • Personal, friendly service
  • 24/7 support wherever you travel
